   - REPEAT_EDGE_DAMPING and fan formula may under/over-penalize common topologies relative to the reference.

4) Temporal-factor semantics (Temporal)
   - _temporal_factor reduces cycle mass linearly toward TEMPORAL_FLOOR; reference may have different shape or opposite direction (reward long-lived cycles).
